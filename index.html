عافیہ Happy Birthday, Afiyah

Allah tumhe hamesha khush rakhe, aur tumhari har dua qubool kare.

Aisha's wedding

The day I noticed your funny, joyful side

You were standing with a plate of rose petals, waiting for the bride to walk in.

She took so long that you gave up and told your cousins to throw the petals on you instead.

Everyone laughed, and I smiled too. That funny, joyful side of you is what I liked the most, and I still do.

The bride was late, so today all the petals are yours.

How we got here

  1. Two days before

    I saw you at the Haldi function. That's when I first liked you.

  2. Aisha's wedding

    And then the wedding. After that, you stayed on my mind.

  3. Ariba appi

    I asked your cousin for help, and she became our bridge.

  4. The first message

    I told you I saw you at Aisha's wedding. You replied, Jii, I was told.

  5. So much in common

    We talked about so many things, our food, our favourite movies and how we see life. Our choices turned out to be so similar.

  6. Some confusions

    We misunderstood each other a few times, and you took some time to think. You were honest with me through all of it, and in the end it only brought us closer.

  7. Next

    Sending the rishta to your home, with both our families. In sha Allah.

A few things I like about you

  • Your funny side

    You make me laugh, even over chat.

  • Your honesty

    You tell me how you really feel, even when it isn't easy to say.

  • Your deen

    You care about doing things the right way. It makes me want to be better too.

  • The questions you ask

    One of the first real questions you asked me was how I handle hard times. That told me a lot about you.

What your name means

Your name is a dua

Afiyah means well-being. Good health, safety and peace of heart, all in one word.

So on your birthday, my first dua for you is your own name. And a few more:

  • Sukoon in your heart, always.
  • Good health, and lots of reasons to smile.
  • Barakah in your studies. May BCA go easy on you.
  • May every dua you make this year be accepted.
  • And may Allah write for you whatever is best for you.

Ameen.

Afiyah,

I don't know what this year will bring. But I'm really grateful that I got to know you.

I'm making dua for you every day. And the rest, I'll handle. The right way, In sha Allah.

Enjoy your day. You deserve every bit of it.

Musheer
